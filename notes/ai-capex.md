# AI CapEx

Reviewed September 8, 2026. Separate actual cash spending, company-defined capex including finance leases, management guidance, and research scenarios. None of these measures automatically equals AI-only spending or deployed accelerator capacity.

## Verified Microsoft Update

The [FY2026 Q4 earnings transcript](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/TranscriptFY26Q4.docx), linked from the [July 29 earnings release](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/Investor/earnings/FY-2026-Q4/press-release-webcast), distinguishes the following:

| Period | Measure | Amount | Classification |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Quarter ended June 30, 2026 | Company-defined capex | Approximately $41B | Actual, includes finance leases |
| Quarter ended June 30, 2026 | Finance leases | $5.6B | Actual, primarily large datacenter sites; included in capex |
| Quarter ended June 30, 2026 | Cash paid for PP&E | $35.8B | Actual cash measure; not additive to the capex total |
| FY2026 ended June 30 | Cash additions to PP&E | $115.948B | Full-year actual from the cash-flow statement |
| Calendar 2026 | Company-defined capex expectation | Approximately $175B | Management outlook after lease reclassification |
| FY2027 Q1 ending September 30 | Company-defined capex expectation | Over $50B | Quarterly management outlook; includes the classification impact |

Management explained that updated useful-life assumptions would move more future datacenter leases from finance leases to operating leases. Finance leases count in its capex measure; operating leases do not. It said calendar-2026 investment expectations were unchanged outside this accounting impact. **Do not interpret the $175B expectation as evidence of a proportional reduction in physical investment.** The source's rounded quarterly components need not add exactly to its rounded capex headline.

## Track Investment Conversion

For each future update, capture company, release date, exact period, cash PP&E, finance-lease additions, operating-lease commitments, total versus AI-only scope, actual versus guidance, and the source URL. Record assumption changes separately from accounting reclassifications.

Pair spending with cloud margins, backlog conversion, installed capacity, and supplier earnings. Supplier sales and a customer's capex are different views of the same investment chain, not amounts to add together. See the [September buildout update](ai-buildout-2026-09.md).
